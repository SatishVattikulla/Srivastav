Hey guys, so many are believing that America has introduced 18% tariffs. This tariff will be paid by American customers. They are the ones who is going to foot the bill and it's not the Indian exporters that will get hurt. This is pure economic madness. It doesn't work this way. Let me help you understand by using the example of GST. Now, on paper GST says that hey, the price of GST is borne by the customers, right? Shopkeeper is not getting hurt because 18% GST or 15% GST, whatever is being charged by the government, the shopkeeper is supposed to collect from you and pay to the government. But think about it logically that if an item cost 100 rupees and there is a sudden increase by 15-20% on that item, is the producer not losing anything or is the shopkeeper not losing anything? Well, they are because the demand of that item is likely to come down. Now, this happens with almost any other good also that if for example 18% tariffs are introduced on Indian goods, the producers of those Indian goods will take some part of that tax on themselves and they will they might increase a little bit of their price of the product to reflect it on customers if they can, but a large part of that tax is borne by the Indian producer only and this is what people miss when they start this trade war debate.
